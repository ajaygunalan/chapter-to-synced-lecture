# Sources and history

Not read during a run; kept for the record.

## What the teaching contract rests on

Mayer, *Multimedia Learning* (modality, coherence, signaling, segmenting,
redundancy); Sweller et al. 2019 (cognitive load, worked examples); Rosenshine
2012 (small steps, questions, models, guided practice); Hundhausen, Douglas &
Stasko 2002 and Naps et al. 2002 (engagement, not visuals, predicts learning
from algorithm visualization); Szpunar et al. 2013 (questions between segments
halve mind-wandering); Muller et al. 2008 (misconception first); Chi & Wylie
2014 (ICAP); Willingham (memory is the residue of thought); Sanderson, Azad,
Victor, Case, Lockhart, Gowers, Tao, Winston, Glass, NPR Training, Metz,
Erickson, Dorst and Todd on the craft.

## How three test chapters classified (slides.md)

- **Skiena, weighted graphs** — procedure and argument, with a
  representation (matching → flow), a transformation (the three lines that
  turn Prim into Dijkstra), six Stop-and-Think exercises, and war stories as
  voice-only case studies. Frames computed by running each algorithm on the
  chapter's own Figure 6.3 graph; a graph invented only where the author
  gives none (Floyd).
- **Martin, SOLID principles** — almost no code. Design evolution,
  architecture, and dependency overlay: UML that passes through four
  versions, one diagram annotated across three subsections, control-flow
  arrows against dependency arrows.
- **Dorst et al., conformal model** — derivation and representation are
  most of it; definitions are equations; two sections are genuine geometric
  constructions (double reflection, screw decomposition); ten drills, ten
  structural exercises, four programming examples.

## What the text layer did on those chapters (extraction.md)

The contraction operator `⌋` deleted at every occurrence; `≠` deleted (so
`−∞·p ≠ 0` read `−∞·p = 0`, and pseudocode `if (a ≠ b)` read `if (a = b)`);
primes, hats and tildes dropped; `½p²` → `12 p2`; `★` → `夹`, `φ` → `␾`;
superscript `-1` floating to the next line; bold vs italic lost, so a
Euclidean vector and a conformal point became the same letter.
